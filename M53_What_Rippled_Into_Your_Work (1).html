        Debrief

        By the time it reaches you, it is already old

        Why it grows on the way to you

        1. 1Stock and spare time absorb the first few days, so nobody notices.
        2. 2The gap keeps growing while it is hidden.
        3. 3When it finally shows, it shows all at once.
        4. 4So the reaction is late and too strong, and lands on somebody else.

        You are rarely reacting to what is happening. You are reacting to what happened.

        What to do with that

        • Ask how old it is A four-week-old problem does not need a four-week-old answer.
        • Say something in week one Being early is worth more than being certain.
        • Wait before you correct again Most over-corrections come from acting twice, before the first action has arrived.
